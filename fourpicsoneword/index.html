Four Pics One Word – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026. This policy applies to the Four Pics One Word Android app (package com.nykloo.fourpicsoneword) published by Nick Jugganaikloo ("we").

Summary

The app has no user accounts, app backend, advertising, or analytics. It does not send your progress or puzzle content to us or to an app server. Game progress and any puzzles you import are kept on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress: the IDs of solved puzzles and your in-game coin balance are stored in the app's private storage so progress is available when you return.
  • Imported puzzles: if you choose a puzzle file through Android's system file picker, the app reads it and stores the puzzle data, including its clue images, in the app's private storage for gameplay. The app does not upload that file to us or to an app server. The file provider you choose may have its own privacy practices.

Collection and sharing

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, or puzzle data. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. The app uses no app backend and makes no network requests. The bundled puzzle content and its photo credits are included with the app.

Retention and deletion

Progress and imported puzzles remain in the app's private storage until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Android backup is disabled for the app. We do not maintain a server-side copy.

Children and other users

This policy describes the app's data practices for all users. The app does not ask for account details or collect or transmit personal information. It does not use targeted advertising or profiling.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date at this address.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub and mention Four Pics One Word.
