Daily Horoscope – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026. This policy applies to the Daily Horoscope Android app (package com.nykloo.dailyhoroscope) published by Nick Jugganaikloo ("we").

Summary

Daily Horoscope has no user accounts and no application backend. Your profile and health-derived summary stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is the location query needed to fetch current weather, if you choose to use that feature. The app is for entertainment and reflection. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Data the app uses

  • Birthday (required): used to calculate your sun sign. Stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Approximate location (optional): if you tap "Use my location", the app requests approximate (coarse) location permission, reads your position once, and sends the latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to retrieve current temperature, weather condition and UV index. Location is not stored on our servers because we have none.
  • ZIP or postal code (optional): if you enter one instead, the text you enter is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find coordinates, which are then used for the weather request as above.
  • Health Connect data (optional): if you choose "Connect Health Connect" and grant permission, the app reads, on your device, only sleep sessions and exercise sessions from the most recent 30 days. From these it computes a simple non-numeric wellbeing summary that adjusts the tone of the horoscope text. Raw health records are never displayed, uploaded, shared, sold, or used for advertising, and are not transmitted to any server. You can revoke the permission at any time in Health Connect or Android settings.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. The only third party that receives data is Open-Meteo, which receives the coordinates (or ZIP/postal code text) described above, together with the technical information inherent in any internet request such as your IP address. See Open-Meteo's own privacy policy at open-meteo.com.

Retention and deletion

Data stored by the app remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, which deletes it. Because we operate no server that stores your data, we hold no copy that we could provide or delete.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS. App data is stored in the app's private sandbox, and Android backup is disabled for the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
